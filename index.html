VideoListo

Turns a long Spanish video into subtitles plus a ready-to-post title, description, chapter timestamps and tags, with one command. Built first for my own use, aimed at Spanish-speaking creators.

What I learned: setting up a GPU environment (CUDA builds of PyTorch, ffmpeg), keeping API keys in .env, and measuring speed and cost instead of guessing.

VideoBusca

A searchable knowledge base for Spanish videos, built on VideoListo's subtitles. Ask a question across many videos and get an answer that cites the source video and the exact timestamp. If the material doesn't support an answer, it says so.

What I learned: how embeddings and a vector store fit together in a RAG system, and why it needs an evaluation set. The numbers showed citations are reliable, and answer accuracy is what to improve next.

ClipES

Brings VideoListo and VideoBusca together into one app for Spanish AI explainer videos: ask a question, get an answer with its source, and jump straight to that moment in the video.

How it was made: built mostly through AI-assisted ("vibe") coding, as an experiment in how fast an idea can become a working full-stack app.
